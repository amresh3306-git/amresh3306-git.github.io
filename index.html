About

Amresh is a Senior Technical Writer with 14+ years of experience delivering clear, user-focused documentation for complex technical products — spanning SaaS platforms, enterprise software, hardware, aerospace, and, most recently, AI-driven aquaculture technology.

His work covers the full documentation lifecycle: user guides, admin guides, installation guides, API documentation, release notes, and standard operating procedures. He builds this content using DITA XML and topic-based authoring to keep it structured, reusable, and consistent across large product lines, and has shipped documentation for government-facing software used by public services in England and Scotland.

He works comfortably at the intersection of engineering and end users — gathering requirements directly from SMEs, product owners, and Agile teams, then translating that into documentation that holds up under real use, in both online and printed form.

★ award-winning improvement

Engineers on-site were running quality checklists entirely on paper: print the checklist, fill it out by hand, scan it, then upload and attach it for review — for every single check. Amresh designed a digital PDF form checklist in Arbortext Editor that engineers could fill in and submit directly, cutting out the print–scan–upload cycle entirely. The improvement was recognized with a formal award from the team in the UK.
